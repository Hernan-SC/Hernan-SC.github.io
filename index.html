About Me

I am a PhD student in Astronomy at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. I completed my Bachelor's degree in Astronomy in 2024. My research is driven by a deep curiosity about how worlds come to be.

My primary goal is to pursue a career in understanding planet formation by engaging in research on planetary systems and exploring the chemistry of planet forming regions.

Throughout my academic path, I have gained extensive experience working with various instruments and global surveys, including TESS, SPECULOOS, LCOGT, and HARPS.
