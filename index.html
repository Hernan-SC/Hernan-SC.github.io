About Me

I am a PhD student in Astronomy at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. My research is driven by a deep curiosity about the fundamental processes that govern the universe and how worlds come to be.

My primary goal is to pursue a career in understanding planet formation by engaging in research on planetary systems and exploring the complex chemistry of planet-forming regions.

Research Areas

Gaseous planets around M-dwarf stars (GEMS) Study of gas giant planets orbiting low mass stars, focusing on their formation and evolution through the transit method. Chemistry of FU Orionis objects Investigation of the chemical composition of young stellar objects undergoing accretion outbursts using infrared observations.
